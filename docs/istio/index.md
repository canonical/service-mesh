# Charmed Istio ambient

Charmed Istio ambient is an opinionated deployment of Istio's [Ambient Mode](https://istio.io/latest/docs/ambient/overview/) using [Juju](http://juju.is/). It gives you mutual TLS, fine-grained authorization, and traffic observability across your microservices without modifying your applications.
